What is Warm Autumn?

Warm Autumn is one of the three Autumn seasons and sits between Warm Spring and True Autumn on the seasonal flow chart. This season combines rich depth with intense warmth, creating a glowing, golden appearance.

If you're a Warm Autumn, the primary characteristic of your overall appearance is warmth, with richness as the secondary aspect. This means warm colors suit you best, and they should be rich and saturated to harmonize with your golden coloring.

When you look in the mirror, you notice the golden, warm quality of your coloring - there's a rich, earthy warmth to your skin, eyes, and hair. Your features have strong golden and copper undertones that create a warm, autumnal appearance.

Your Characteristics

Eyes

Warm Autumn eyes are rich and warm in color. They typically come in warm brown, amber, hazel with golden flecks, olive green, or warm teal. Your eyes have a distinctive golden or copper quality, often with a sunburst pattern radiating from the pupil. The whites of your eyes may have a warm, creamy tone rather than stark white.

Skin

Warm Autumn skin ranges from light ivory to deep bronze and always has warm, golden undertones. Your skin has a rich quality and may have peachy, golden, or bronze tones. Gold jewelry is significantly more flattering than silver. You may tan easily and develop a warm, golden glow. Many Warm Autumns have freckles or warm-toned beauty marks.

Hair

True to the Warm Autumn warmth, your hair is naturally rich and golden. It ranges from golden blonde, copper, auburn, to warm golden brown or chestnut. Whatever the shade, your hair has intense golden, red, or copper undertones and natural highlights. Your hair may lighten with golden tones in the sun.

Contrast

Warm Autumn is characterized by medium contrast between skin, hair, and eyes. There's enough variation to create interest, but everything exists within the warm, golden spectrum. The overall effect is harmonious and rich rather than dramatic.

Warm Autumn vs Warm Spring

Warm Autumns are often confused with Warm Springs since both seasons share warmth as their primary characteristic. The key difference lies in their secondary aspect: Warm Autumn colors are rich and earthy, while Warm Spring colors are clear and bright.

Warm Autumn

  • Rich, saturated colors
  • Earthy, muted quality
  • Deep golden undertones
  • Best in burnt oranges, mustards
  • Colors have depth and complexity

Warm Spring

  • Clear, vibrant colors
  • Bright, fresh quality
  • Light golden undertones
  • Best in coral, peach, bright yellows
  • Colors are clean and luminous

If you're still unsure, compare rust (Warm Autumn) and coral (Warm Spring) against your face. Warm Autumns look rich and harmonious in earthy rust, while bright coral can look overpowering. Warm Springs glow in clear coral but look dull in muted rust.
