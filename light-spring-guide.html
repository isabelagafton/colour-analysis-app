What is Light Spring?

Light Spring is one of the three Spring seasons and sits between True Spring and Light Summer on the seasonal flow chart. This season combines the freshness of Spring with a touch of Summer's softness, creating a delicate, luminous appearance.

If you're a Light Spring, the primary characteristic of your overall appearance is lightness, with warmth as the secondary aspect. This means warm colors suit you better than cool ones, but the colors must remain light and gentle to harmonize with your delicate coloring.

When you look in the mirror, the first thing you notice is how light your overall coloring is - there's very little contrast between your skin, eyes, and hair. All of your features are similarly light, creating a cohesive, delicate appearance.

Your Characteristics

Eyes

Light Spring eyes are naturally light in color. They typically come in light blue, green, hazel, or light brown. Like all Spring eyes, they have a bright quality with warm undertones. You might notice the signature Spring sunburst pattern radiating from the pupil on the iris.

Skin

Light Spring skin ranges from fair to medium and has either neutral or neutral-warm undertones. This means both gold and silver may look decent against your skin, but gold flatters you more. Your skin may have golden, peachy, or ivory undertones. Light Springs often have freckles, particularly across the nose and cheeks.

Hair

True to the Light Spring primary aspect, your hair is naturally light. It ranges from light to medium golden blonde, strawberry blonde, light copper, to light golden brown. Whatever the shade, your hair has warm, golden undertones or natural highlights. Most Light Springs were blonde as children, and the hair may have darkened slightly with age but retained its warm tone.

Contrast

Light Spring is characterized by very low contrast between skin, hair, and eyes. Even the contrast between your iris and the whites of your eyes is relatively low. There are no dramatically dark areas anywhere in your coloring - everything exists in a similar, light value range.

Light Spring vs Light Summer

Light Springs are often confused with Light Summers since both seasons share lightness as their primary characteristic and both have low contrast. The key difference lies in their secondary aspect: Light Spring is warm, while Light Summer is cool.

Light Spring

  • Warm undertones (golden, peachy)
  • Clear, bright appearance
  • Hair has golden highlights
  • Gold jewelry is more flattering
  • Warm, vibrant colors suit best

Light Summer

  • Cool undertones (pink, rosy)
  • Soft, muted appearance
  • Hair has ashy tones
  • Silver jewelry is more flattering
  • Cool, softer colors suit best

If you're still unsure, try holding up gold and silver jewelry to your face in natural light. Light Springs will look healthier and more radiant in gold, while cool metals can make you appear washed out or tired.
