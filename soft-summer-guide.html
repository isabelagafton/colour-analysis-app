What is Soft Summer?

Soft Summer is one of the three Summer seasons and sits between True Summer and Soft Autumn on the seasonal flow chart. Unlike Soft Autumn, this season is cool. However, both sister seasons are a blend of Autumn and Summer, with Soft Autumn leaning more towards Autumn, and Soft Summer leaning more towards Summer.

If you're a Soft Summer, the primary characteristic of your overall appearance is mutedness, with coolness as the secondary aspect. When you look in the mirror, the first thing you notice is that your skin, eyes and hair all blend into one another. Your features appear somewhat "greyed out" or toned down, giving you a soft, muted appearance.

The contrast between your skin, eyes and hair is low to medium. But the important thing is that your coloring blends and is not intense. In addition, your coloring has cooler undertones, meaning silver flatters your skin more than gold.

Your Characteristics

Eyes

Soft Summer eyes can be grey, grey blue, grey green or grey hazel. A muted brown is common for darker ethnicities. Regardless of color, Soft Summer eyes are always softly greyed. Although they are clearer and less muddy than Soft Autumn eyes, they are by no means bright. You may notice a crackled glass pattern on the iris typical of Summer eyes.

Skin

Soft Summer skin is either neutral, olive or neutral-cool with ashy undertones and possibly a pink tinge. This means both silver and gold look good against the skin, but silver looks better. Skin tones range from fair to tan, but they are muted rather than vibrant. Soft Summers may also have freckles.

Hair

Hair in this color season ranges from medium ash blonde over light to medium ash brown. Because of the high concentration of grey pigments, Soft Summer hair is always muted and ashy, never saturated and bright. When exposed to the sun, it can develop ash blonde highlights.

Contrast

Depending on the hair/skin combination, the contrast level between the features is low to medium. The coloring remains around a medium value with no truly light or dark areas. Everything blends together in a harmonious, soft way.

Soft Summer vs Soft Autumn

Containing the most muted and toned-down of the Summer family's colors, Soft Summers may very easily be mistaken for Soft Autumns. Both color seasons are very similar, and the distinctions are subtle. Both seasons' primary color aspect is muted, but while Soft Autumn is warm, Soft Summer is cool.

Soft Summer

  • Cool undertones (grey, ashy)
  • Muted, greyed-out appearance
  • High content of grey and olive pigments
  • Eyes can appear greyish
  • Cool, muted colors suit best

Soft Autumn

  • Warm undertones (walnut, honey)
  • Muted, toned-down appearance
  • Walnut, beige, and gold pigments
  • Eyes remain green, hazel, or olive
  • Warm, muted colors suit best

If you're still unsure, try holding something grey to your face in natural daylight without makeup. If your eyes seem greyish (even if they have greenish or brown blotches), you're likely a Soft Summer. If they remain green, hazel, or olive, you lean more towards Soft Autumn.
