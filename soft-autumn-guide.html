What is Soft Autumn?

Soft Autumn is one of the three Autumn seasons and sits between Soft Summer and True Autumn on the seasonal flow chart. This season combines the gentleness of Summer with the warmth of Autumn, creating a muted, harmonious appearance.

If you're a Soft Autumn, the primary characteristic of your overall appearance is softness, with warmth as the secondary aspect. This means warm colors suit you best, but they must be muted and gentle to harmonize with your delicate coloring.

When you look in the mirror, you notice the overall softness and low contrast of your coloring - there's no starkness anywhere. Your features have subtle, dusty warm undertones that blend together beautifully, creating a gentle, muted appearance.

Your Characteristics

Eyes

Soft Autumn eyes are muted and gentle in color. They typically come in soft hazel, olive green, soft brown, or muted grey-green. Your eyes have a dusty, gentle quality without much brightness. The colors seem to blend together softly, and you may notice a subtle, warm undertone rather than stark definition.

Skin

Soft Autumn skin ranges from light to medium and has neutral-warm undertones. Your skin has a gentle quality - not too golden, not too peachy - just softly warm. Both gold and silver may look okay on you, but gold complements you slightly better. You may have freckles or moles with warm, muted tones. Your skin lacks the high contrast or stark quality found in other seasons.

Hair

True to the Soft Autumn softness, your hair is naturally muted. It ranges from soft ash blonde, mousy brown, to medium ash brown with subtle, dusty warm highlights. Your hair doesn't have the golden intensity of Warm Autumn or the richness of Deep Autumn - instead, it has a gentle, blended quality. Your natural hair color lacks strong contrast or vividness.

Contrast

Soft Autumn is characterized by very low contrast between skin, hair, and eyes. Everything blends together harmoniously with no dramatic differences. The overall effect is gentle and understated rather than striking or vivid.

Soft Autumn vs Soft Summer

Soft Autumns are often confused with Soft Summers since both seasons share softness as their primary characteristic and both have low contrast. The key difference lies in their secondary aspect: Soft Autumn is warm, while Soft Summer is cool.

Soft Autumn

  • Warm undertones (golden, peachy)
  • Muted, earthy quality
  • Dusty warm tones
  • Best in warm earth tones
  • Gold jewelry is more flattering

Soft Summer

  • Cool undertones (pink, rosy)
  • Muted, soft quality
  • Dusty cool tones
  • Best in cool pastels
  • Silver jewelry is more flattering

If you're still unsure, compare sage green (Soft Autumn) and powder blue (Soft Summer) against your face. Soft Autumns look harmonious in warm sage, while cool powder blue can look discordant. Soft Summers glow in cool powder blue but look sallow in warm sage.
