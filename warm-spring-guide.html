What is Warm Spring?

Warm Spring is one of the three Spring seasons and sits between True Spring and Warm Autumn on the seasonal flow chart. This season combines the clarity of Spring with the richness of Autumn, creating a warm, vibrant appearance.

If you're a Warm Spring, the primary characteristic of your overall appearance is warmth, with brightness as the secondary aspect. This means warm colors suit you better than cool ones, and the colors should have a clear, saturated quality to complement your vibrant coloring.

When you look in the mirror, the first thing you notice is how warm your overall coloring is - golden, peachy, or coppery tones dominate your skin, hair, and eyes. There's a sun-kissed, radiant quality to your appearance.

Your Characteristics

Eyes

Warm Spring eyes typically come in warm shades like golden brown, amber, hazel with golden flecks, or warm green. They have a clear, bright quality with golden or bronze undertones. You might notice warm highlights or a sunburst pattern radiating from the pupil.

Skin

Warm Spring skin ranges from fair to medium with strong warm, golden undertones. Your skin may have peachy, golden, or bronze tones. Many Warm Springs have freckles, especially when exposed to sun. You tan easily and beautifully, taking on a golden glow rather than burning.

Hair

True to the Warm Spring primary aspect, your hair has rich, warm tones. It ranges from golden blonde, strawberry blonde, auburn, copper, to warm golden brown or chestnut. Your hair likely has natural golden, red, or copper highlights that catch the light beautifully in the sun.

Contrast

Warm Spring is characterized by medium contrast between skin, hair, and eyes. Unlike Light Spring's delicate coloring, your features have more depth and saturation while maintaining that warm, golden quality throughout.

Warm Spring vs Warm Autumn

Warm Springs are often confused with Warm Autumns since both seasons share warmth as their primary characteristic. The key difference lies in their secondary aspect: Warm Spring is bright and clear, while Warm Autumn is muted and soft.

Warm Spring

  • Warm undertones (golden, peachy)
  • Clear, vibrant appearance
  • Hair has golden, copper highlights
  • Gold jewelry is flattering
  • Bright, saturated colors suit best

Warm Autumn

  • Warm undertones (golden, bronze)
  • Muted, earthy appearance
  • Hair has deep, rich tones
  • Gold and bronze jewelry flattering
  • Muted, earthy colors suit best

If you're still unsure, try holding up a bright coral versus a muted terracotta. Warm Springs will look vibrant and healthy in bright coral, while muted colors can make you appear tired or washed out.
