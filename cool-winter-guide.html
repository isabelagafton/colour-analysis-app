What is Cool Winter?

Cool Winter is the purest and most quintessential Winter season in the 12-season system. Sometimes called True Winter, this season sits at the cool extreme of the Winter palette and represents the archetype of what Winter coloring truly is.

If you're a Cool Winter, you have the most striking and dramatic natural coloring. Your primary characteristic is coolness - your coloring is completely devoid of warmth. The secondary aspect is high contrast, creating a bold, crisp appearance that can handle the clearest, most vivid colors.

When you look in the mirror, you'll notice the sharp contrast between your features - perhaps dark hair against pale skin, or striking eyes that stand out dramatically. There's nothing muted or soft about your coloring - it's bold, clear, and undeniably cool-toned.

Your Characteristics

Eyes

Cool Winter eyes are striking and clear. They come in cool shades of blue, blue-gray, violet, or deep brown - always with icy or cool undertones. The contrast between the iris and the white of the eye is typically high, adding to the dramatic quality. You might notice a crisp, clear quality to your eyes with no golden or warm flecks.

Skin

Cool Winter skin has distinctly cool undertones with absolutely no golden or peachy warmth. Your complexion ranges from very fair porcelain to deep olive or dark brown, but always with blue, pink, or neutral-cool undertones. Silver jewelry flatters you far more than gold. Your skin may have rosy, pink, or even bluish undertones, and you likely tan with cool olive or rosy tones rather than golden.

Hair

Cool Winter hair is typically dark with cool undertones - think blue-black, dark ash brown, salt-and-pepper gray, or pure white. If you have lighter hair, it will have ash or platinum tones rather than golden warmth. Your hair has no red, golden, or warm highlights - any dimension comes from cool, ashy tones.

Contrast

Cool Winter features high contrast between skin, hair, and eyes. This creates a dramatic, striking appearance - perhaps very pale skin with dark hair, or deep skin with bright white hair. Even the contrast between your iris and the whites of your eyes is noticeably high, giving you that piercing, clear-eyed quality.

Cool Winter vs Bright Winter

Cool Winters and Bright Winters are both part of the Winter family and share cool undertones, but they differ in their secondary characteristics. Cool Winter is purely cool with high contrast, while Bright Winter has a slight warm influence from Spring, creating even more vibrant, electric colors.

Cool Winter

  • Purely cool undertones (no warmth)
  • Classic, sophisticated appearance
  • Best in true, icy colors
  • Silver is ideal
  • Can wear soft pastels alongside jewel tones

Bright Winter

  • Cool with slight warm influence
  • Electric, vibrant appearance
  • Best in neon-bright colors
  • Silver or white metals
  • Needs maximum saturation and brightness

If neon pink or electric blue look overwhelming on you, you're likely a Cool Winter. If those ultra-bright colors make you come alive and look more vibrant, you might be a Bright Winter instead.
