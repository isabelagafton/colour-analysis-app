What is Cool Summer?

Cool Summer (also known as True Summer) is the original Summer season in the four seasons colour analysis and sits between Light Summer and Soft Summer on the seasonal flow chart. Its opposite season is True Winter. Both are cool, but True Winter is dark and bright; whereas Cool Summer is light and muted.

If you're a Cool Summer, the primary characteristic of your overall appearance is coolness, with softness as the secondary aspect. When you look in the mirror, the first thing you notice is the obvious cool quality of your coloring. Your skin, eyes and hair all have cool undertones - blue or beige - with no warmth anywhere.

In addition, your features are muted, meaning they blend rather than contrast each other. Compared to the other two Summer seasons, Cool Summer is the most contrasted, with a medium level of contrast between features.

Your Characteristics

Eyes

Cool Summer eyes are usually blue, grey, green or light grey hazel. Darker ethnicities may have greyish brown eyes. Regardless of color, Cool Summer eyes are always softly greyed, making them muted and less vibrant than sparkling Spring or Winter eyes. You may notice a crackled glass pattern on the iris typical of Summer eyes.

Skin

Cool Summer skin is always cool, with blue or beige undertones and possibly a pink tinge. It ranges from fair to tan. Since Cool Summer skin is cool, silver looks good against it, but gold makes it appear off or washed out.

Hair

The hair can be any shade from dark blonde, over light and medium brown to dark brown. What defines Cool Summer hair is the absence of brightness - instead, it's ashy and muted. There is no warmth in the hair, meaning very little to no red undertones, no golden glow, and usually no natural highlights.

Contrast

Due to the combination of darker hair with lighter skin, the level of contrast between a Cool Summer's features is medium. This makes Cool Summer the season with the highest contrast out of the three Summer types, though it's still softer and more blended than Winter's high contrast.

Cool Summer vs True Winter

Being the coolest of the Summer family, Cool Summers can sometimes be mistaken for Winters. Both are cool seasons, but while True Winter is dark and contrasting, Cool Summer is lighter and softer. The high-contrasting colors (including black) of Winter are overpowering on Cool Summers.

Cool Summer

  • Cool, muted appearance
  • Medium contrast
  • Ashy, greyed hair
  • Soft, blended features
  • Muted, gentle colors suit best

True Winter

  • Cool, bright appearance
  • High contrast
  • Dark, vivid hair
  • Sharp, defined features
  • Intense, icy colors suit best

If you're still unsure, try wearing true black near your face. Cool Summers will look aged and washed out in black, while it enhances Winters. Cool Summers shine in soft greys, dusty blues, and muted tones.
