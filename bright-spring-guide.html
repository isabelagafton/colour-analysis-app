What is Bright Spring?

Bright Spring is one of the three Spring seasons and sits between Bright Winter and True Spring on the seasonal flow chart. This season combines the intensity of Winter with the warmth of Spring, creating a vibrant, electric appearance.

If you're a Bright Spring, the primary characteristic of your overall appearance is brightness, with warmth as the secondary aspect. This means bright, saturated colors suit you best, and they should have warm undertones to complement your vibrant coloring.

When you look in the mirror, the first thing you notice is how saturated and vibrant your overall coloring is. There is no greyness in any of your features - your appearance is highly contrasted and electric. Your eyes probably stand out and may even sparkle.

Your Characteristics

Eyes

Bright Spring eyes are generally clear and bright. Because of the high contrast with the whites of the eye, they typically stand out. They can be blue, green, topaz or even brown as long as they are bright. Bright Spring eyes have warm undertones, and you may notice a sunburst pattern on the iris, which is typical for Spring eyes.

Skin

Bright Spring skin comes in a broad range of shades: from fair to tan. It has either neutral or neutral-warm undertones - meaning both gold and silver look good against it, but gold looks somewhat better. Your skin may have golden, peachy, or ivory tones.

Hair

Like the skin, the hair is typically warmer and tends to develop highlights when exposed to the sun. Medium golden to dark golden blonde with highlights are very common. But copper, auburn and medium brown with reddish undertones are also possible.

Contrast

Bright Spring has one of the highest contrast levels between skin, hair and eyes among the twelve colour seasons. There is even a high contrast between the iris and the whites of the eye. This is because brightness creates contrast.

Bright Spring vs Bright Winter

Bright Springs are often confused with Bright Winters since both seasons are highly contrasted and vibrant. The key difference lies in their temperature: Bright Spring has warm undertones, while Bright Winter has cool undertones.

Bright Spring

  • Neutral-warm undertones (golden)
  • Vibrant, electric appearance
  • Hair has golden, copper highlights
  • Gold jewelry is most flattering
  • Warm, saturated colors suit best

Bright Winter

  • Cool undertones (blue-tinted)
  • Stark, icy appearance
  • Hair has ash or cool tones
  • Silver jewelry is most flattering
  • Cool, saturated colors suit best

If you're still unsure, try holding up a warm turquoise versus an icy blue. Bright Springs will look vibrant and healthy in warm turquoise, while icy blues can make you appear washed out or harsh.
