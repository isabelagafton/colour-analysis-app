What is Light Summer?

Light Summer is one of the three Summer seasons and sits between Light Spring and True Summer on the seasonal flow chart. Unlike Light Spring, this season is cool. However, both sister seasons are a blend of Spring and Summer, with Light Spring leaning more towards the Spring palette and Light Summer leaning more towards the Summer palette.

If you're a Light Summer, the primary characteristic of your overall appearance is lightness, with coolness as the secondary aspect. This means cool colors suit you better than warm ones, but the colors must remain light and gentle to harmonize with your delicate coloring.

When you look in the mirror, the first thing you notice is the low contrast between your skin, eyes, and hair. All of your features are very light. In addition, your features have mainly cool (blue and grey) undertones, meaning silver flatters you more than gold.

Your Characteristics

Eyes

Light Summer eyes are naturally light in color. They typically come in light grey, light blue, light azure, or light green. There is no darkness about them, and they have a cool, greyish undertone. Typical of the Summer eye, you may notice a crackled glass pattern and a soft separation between the iris and the whites of the eye.

Skin

Light Summer skin ranges from fair to medium and has either neutral or neutral-cool undertones with beige or pinkish tones. You know you have neutral-cool skin if both gold and silver look good on you, but silver looks better. Light Summers have delicate, soft skin with no strong contrast.

Hair

True to the Light Summer primary aspect, your hair is naturally light. It ranges from icy light to medium ash blonde or light ash brown. Typically, the hair does not have natural highlights. Many Light Summers were blonde as children, but their hair has darkened with age while retaining cool, ashy tones.

Contrast

Light Summer is characterized by very low contrast between skin, hair, and eyes. Even the contrast between your iris and the whites of your eyes is relatively low. There are no dramatically dark areas anywhere in your coloring - everything exists in a similar, light value range with cool undertones.

Light Summer vs Light Spring

Light Summers are often confused with Light Springs since both seasons share lightness as their primary characteristic and both have low contrast. The key difference lies in their secondary aspect: Light Summer is cool, while Light Spring is warm.

Light Summer

  • Cool undertones (pink, beige, grey)
  • Soft, muted appearance
  • Hair has ashy tones
  • Silver jewelry is more flattering
  • Cool, gentle colors suit best

Light Spring

  • Warm undertones (golden, peachy)
  • Clear, bright appearance
  • Hair has golden highlights
  • Gold jewelry is more flattering
  • Warm, vibrant colors suit best

If you're still unsure, try holding up gold and silver jewelry to your face in natural light. Light Summers will look healthier and more radiant in silver, while warm metals can make you appear washed out or sallow.
